# Gujarati AI Writing Assistant

## Vision
Build an AI-powered Gujarati writing platform for grammar, spelling, rewriting and content generation.

## Mission
Help Gujarati users write accurate, professional Gujarati.

## Problem Statement
Gujarati lacks a comprehensive AI writing assistant similar to Grammarly.

## Target Users
- Students
- Teachers
- Journalists
- Politicians
- Government Employees
- Businesses
- Content Writers

## Goals
- Grammar Check
- Spell Check
- AI Rewrite
- Dictionary
- News Writing
- Official Letters
